Your Mission

You are trapped in a haunted house! Solve each bill to unlock the correct door. Find 10% tax and a 10% or 20% tip, both based on the original bill. Add all three amounts to find the final total.

ONE WORKED EXAMPLE
Bill: $20 • Tax: 10% • Tip: 20%
10% of $20 = $2 tax
20% of $20 = $4 tip (double the 10%)
Final total = $20 + $2 + $4 = $26

Type the tax, tip, and total. The correct door will open when your math is right. Wrong answer? Edit it and try again!
